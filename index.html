源于阵发性 (Bursty) 删除操作的文件同步算法 File Synchronization Algorithm from Bursty Deletions 2017.05 – 2018.07

  • 提出用稳定的双态马尔可夫链来建模阵发性删除操作。
  • 基于已有的针对 i.i.d. 删除操作的文件同步算法框架，设计了三个核心模块：匹配模块（使用包含 Dijkstra 算法在内的图论方法检测中心子字符串在删除文件中的正确匹配位置）、阵发性删除恢复模块（采用分而治之与非分而治之两种方案恢复片段子字符串的被删除字符）和 LDPC 解码模块（纠正残余错误）。

  • Proposed modeling bursty deletions using a stationary two-state Markov chain.
  • Designed a three-module architecture consisting of a Matching Module (using graph-theoretic methods including Dijkstra's algorithm to locate anchor substrings), a Bursty Deletion Recovery Module (with divide-and-conquer and non-divide-and-conquer schemes), and an LDPC Decoding Module (for residual error correction).
基于 EM (Expectation Maximization) 的稀疏 RLS (Recursive Least Squares) 算法 EM-Based Sparse Recursive Least Squares (RLS) Algorithm 2017.06 – 2017.12
  • 提出一个新的稀疏 RLS 算法，结合最大似然准则和 EM 算法进行稀疏系统辨识。
  • 采用待估计模型参数的 p 准范数 (p-norm-like) 作为损失函数的正则化项以度量稀疏度（当 p 为 0 或 1 时即对应广泛使用的 l0 和 l1 范数）。
  • 不同于传统途径，将正则化损失函数最小化问题转化为正则化最大似然问题，并通过 EM 算法的 E 步和 M 步推导出模型参数的闭式迭代计算公式，实现高效求解。
  • Proposed a novel sparse RLS algorithm leveraging Maximum Likelihood estimation and the Expectation-Maximization (EM) framework.
  • Employed a p-norm-like constraint as the sparsity-inducing regularization term (which reduces to the widely used l0 and l1 norms when p = 0 or 1).
  • Reformulated the regularized loss minimization into a regularized maximum likelihood problem and derived closed-form iterative updates via the E-step and M-step of the EM algorithm.
